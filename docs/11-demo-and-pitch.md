# 11 · Demo and pitch

Owners: **Dev** (deck + script), **Mo** (recording + edit). Judges score every team from the deck; only the top 3 pitch live, so the deck must stand alone.

## Pitch

**"Start investing in five minutes. Then forget about it. We'll tell you when something needs your decision."**

**The problems:**
1. Most UK adults don't invest (FCA 2024: ~35% hold investments).
2. People who do invest buy and forget.

## Demo storyline (3-minute video, recorded from the hosted link)

1. **0:00** Landing: "Historical demo · As of 7 Jul 2026".
2. **0:10** Quiz (5 answers) → "You're a Growth investor", with an example loss in £ → mandate.
3. **0:30** Build: the agent tree grows (lead → scouts → filing reader). Show a **refused depth-3 spawn**. The gate strip goes green; the critic flags one point; QA passes.
4. **1:00** Proposal: recommended + 2 alternatives, "why this fund", correlation lattice callout. Approve.
5. **1:15** Forget: the timeline plays, quiet fortnightly reviews appear as grey markers, and "maintain" lines fill the digest.
6. **1:35** **An alarming headline arrives → maintain** (the price move was explained; no change).
7. **1:50** A notification arrives: decision card for a real Jul–Nov 2026 event. Tap **Why?** The replay shows the evidence, exposure maths, an **injection quarantined** and a **concentration block** before the final proposal.
8. **2:20** Approve in one tap → order filled next close → rebalances list.
9. **2:30** Chat: "Why did you sell X?" The answer has evidence chips.
10. **2:40** Benchmarks: Gemini agents vs the same engine without agents vs buy-and-hold / global ETF (whole window held-out); "X reviews, Y checks, Z decisions asked of you".
11. **2:55** Switch to **Current markets · Paper portfolio**: same screens, today's data.
